# SUPERPOSITION V1
hi. this is my personal preset that i decided to share. i quite like it and hope you feel the same way. the main focus of this preset is storytelling; taking inspiration from ttrpg design principles, the ai sets the dramatic truth of the plot from the very beginning and keeps it the exact same way until the resolution of an arc, leading to coherent stories that are planned from the beginning. the reason i made this preset is because opus was pissing me off by making a 'story' where nothing ever happened. superposition is here to make sure something happens.

## STRENGTHS
🔴 **AN INTELLIGIBLE STORY:** ai comes up with shit on the spot, so "stories" in roleplay often fail to be compelling, coherent, or fair. superposition solves this by having the ai come up with a 'truth' at the very beginning, which prevents it from spiraling into a constant *deus ex machina*.<br>
🔴 **GENRES AND PACING:** superposition, through pacing and genre toggles, gives structure to the ai's storytelling. it progresses through scenes on its own with conflicts inspired by your chosen genre. the inclusion of breather scenes keep it from feeling like one huge struggle from beginning to end.<br>
🔴 **ENDLESS AND DRIVEN:** when one arc ends, another begins. plus, the ai will actually... drive the story and won't just sit around jerking off via prose.<br>
